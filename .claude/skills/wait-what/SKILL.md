The owner did not follow your last message. Explain it again:

1. Start with the context: what you were doing and why.
2. Say where you are now, and what decision or action comes next.
3. Apply every ASD-STE100 writing rule strictly for this answer: one idea a sentence, active voice, common words.
4. Keep the project's terms from `docs/voice.md`. Define each technical term the first time, or use an everyday
   comparison.
5. Keep every number and condition. Cut words, never facts.
